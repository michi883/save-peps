**Full description**

Two Peps. One little predicament.

Save Peps is a one-tap puzzle game about bringing two tiny friends back together. Study each playful 3D scene, choose the object that will help, and watch the world spring into motion.

- Explore 12 handcrafted rounds, each with its own colorful world and playful rule.
- Solve 36 distinct rescues with simple one-tap choices.
- Learn through funny outcomes and automatic retries—no lives, timers, or penalties.
- Earn a first-try mark for spotting the solution right away.
- Play Rounds 1–10 for free.
- Unlock Rounds 11–12 forever with one optional purchase. No subscription.

Every rescue is short, visual, and made to leave the Peps smiling.
